Phase One

A small resort with a big idea.

The company holds title to a coastal parcel on Koh Phangan, acquired and registered in the early operational phase of Red Sunset KP. The land is held as the operational base for the project — not for passive investment.

The design pathway envisions a small number of villas on a hillside site, designed with restraint. Compact footprint, native landscaping, and renewable energy infrastructure integrated into the structure rather than added later.

The guest experience is shaped around quiet, light, and the materials of the place. A short walk from the coastline. Architecture that gives more than it takes.

Why Koh Phangan

An island ready for the next chapter.

Two business partners. One opportunity that kept showing up.

Red Sunset KP was born from a working partnership on the ground. Founding Director Sirin Nekhamatcha, an established businesswoman based in Koh Phangan, and her strategic partner identified a recurring pattern: an island with growing premium hospitality demand, but ageing energy infrastructure unable to support it. This is the company built to address both.

Energy import dependency

Koh Phangan imports the majority of its electricity via undersea cables from the mainland — a structural vulnerability for any business building here.

Seasonal demand spikes and outages

During the rainy season, electricity demand rises while supply reliability drops. Outages are routine across the island. Distributed solar with storage directly addresses this challenge — for our resort and for the wider community.

Renewable infrastructure gap

Despite ideal solar conditions, distributed renewable infrastructure on the island remains underdeveloped — a meaningful gap for both businesses and residents.

A regional gateway

Koh Phangan sits between Koh Samui and Koh Tao, with established travel and trade flows. Infrastructure built here is positioned to extend across the wider archipelago.

This is both a hospitality opportunity and a contribution to Thailand's energy transition goals.

Phase Two

From one resort to island infrastructure.

The resort is the first step. By designing a property that runs on its own renewable energy infrastructure, we are proving the model that defines our next phase.

Red Sunset KP is developing a roadmap for distributed solar infrastructure across Koh Phangan, starting with our own properties and expanding to community partnerships.

Initial regulatory engagement is underway. The company has begun early-stage dialogue with the Provincial Electricity Authority (PEA) regarding renewable energy integration pathways, and is conducting compliance review with a local Thai architectural consultancy ahead of permit applications.

Our long-term roadmap includes grid integration in cooperation with PEA, subject to regulatory approvals. The company is positioned to engage with Thailand's national frameworks for sustainable investment and renewable energy development.
